What did you ask Copilot to help you build? How did you break down the problem?
    Copilot helped me build a simple console app which is a social media platform with features such as post, like, comment, and delete.
How did your approach to asking questions change as you worked?
    It didn't change very much. I am pretty experienced with prompting AI from my internship, so I already kind of knew how to as questions.
What parts of the development process with GitHub Copilot surprised you?
    I think I was most surprised at the fact that I didn't really have to do trial and error. Usually I have to go back and forth with AI some, but it was very helpful and knew what to do from the start.
What did you learn about the technology you used that you didn't know before?
    I had never used Copilot in VS Code before, so it was nice to know that it is a feature I can use moving forward.
What would you do differently if you had to build this again?
    I think I would add a few more features to it and do things more step by step rather than prompting Copilot to do most of it at once.
